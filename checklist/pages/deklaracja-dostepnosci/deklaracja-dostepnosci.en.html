Accessibility statement

La Petite Bloom online store · LPB Sp. z o.o. · date of publication 24.06.2026

LPB Sp. z o.o. (the La Petite Bloom online store) makes every effort to ensure that the website is accessible to the widest possible range of users, including people with special needs.

Compliance status

We strive for compliance with the WCAG 2.1 standard at level AA. As a micro-enterprise we are not legally obliged to meet the requirements of the Europejski Akt o Dostępności (European Accessibility Act), however we voluntarily take steps to improve accessibility.

Known limitations

Some elements of the website may not yet meet all accessibility criteria. We are working on improving them progressively.

Feedback and contact

Please send comments on accessibility and requests to provide information in an alternative format to: hello@lapetitebloom.com. We will endeavour to resolve the reported problem within a reasonable time.

Date

The statement was drawn up on 24.06.2026; it will be updated as changes are introduced.
