  1. Performance of the order and of the sales contract — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  2. Issuing and storing accounting documents — Article 6(1)(c) GDPR (legal obligation).
  3. Handling Returns and complaints and the pursuit or defence of claims — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).
  4. Maintaining the Customer account — Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  5. Sending the newsletter — Article 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent).
  6. Marketing and statistics/analytics carried out through cookies — on the basis of consent (Article 6(1)(a)) or of legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)), in accordance with the consent settings.

The data are stored on servers within the European Economic Area. Google services may be an exception — where data are transferred to the USA, the transfer takes place on the basis of an adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or of standard contractual clauses (SCC).

7. Rights of the data subject

You have the right to access your data, to have it rectified or erased, to restrict its processing, to data portability, to object, and to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych (President of the Personal Data Protection Office), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa.

8. Handling requests and erasure of data

  1. The request should be submitted to rodo@lapetitebloom.com.

  3. We reply without undue delay, at the latest within 1 month; in complex cases the time limit may be extended to 3 months, of which we will inform you.
  4. Data covered by a legal obligation (e.g. data on invoices) are not erased before the required period has elapsed — during that time their processing is restricted.

11. Voluntary provision of data and absence of profiling

Providing the data is voluntary but necessary in order to conclude and perform the contract. We do not take decisions based solely on automated processing (including profiling) that would produce legal effects concerning the Customer.
